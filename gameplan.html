The 5-day build

Two tracks run in parallel each day: the store gets built while the ads get produced. Everything store-side happens on a draft theme — the live storefront is never touched until launch morning.

D-5 Sat · 5 Jul

Foundation & accounts

Store

  • Duplicate the live theme to a safe draft copy — all build work happens there.
  • Approve & push product copy (title, benefit description, bundle messaging) to the mask. GATE
  • Decide the gift/bundle mechanic — pick a free-gift app or Shopify Function for the pillowcase + free shipping.
  • Create the silk pillowcase as a €0 / hidden gift product.

Ads

  • Stand up Meta Business Suite — Business account, ad account, page, payment method.

Product page & listicle

Store

  • Build the hero product page — image gallery, benefit copy, sticky bundle selector with the 3 tiers.

Ads

  • Set up Canva (free) with a Valoure brand kit — night-indigo palette, one display font, logo.
  • Build the ad template in 3 sizes: 1080×1350 (feed), 1080×1920 (story/reels), 1080×1080.

D-3 Mon · 7 Jul

Static ad production

Ads (main focus today)

  • Produce the 5 static concepts below — each in feed + story size = ~10 files.
  • Keep text under ~20% of the image and inside safe zones for Reels/Stories.

  • Export at high quality, named by angle + size so ad sets stay organized.

Store

  • Build the homepage — hero, benefit strip, bundle, reviews, guarantee, footer.
  • Set the store logo, colors & favicon to match the ads (one consistent look).

02

Your 5 static ad concepts

Built for the racing-mind persona and your bundle offer. Angles 1–3 are the workhorses; 4–5 add social proof and a native feel. Produce each in feed (1080×1350) and story (1080×1920) so it runs across placements.

1Problem · Agitate

The 3 a.m. brain

"Still awake? It's not you — your brain just won't switch off."

Visual: dark bedroom, the glow of a phone or a clock reading 3:00. Restless, relatable.
Job: stop the scroll by naming the exact feeling before showing the product.

2Mechanism · Analogy

A weighted blanket for your eyes

"The calming science of weighted blankets — now made for your eyes."

Visual: clean hero shot of the mask with a soft "gentle weight" cue.
Job: explain how it works so the price feels justified.

3Offer · Value

Buy 2, sleep better together

"Buy 2 → 10% off + free shipping + a free silk pillowcase. 🎁"

Visual: mask + pillowcase gift stacked; bold offer text; "Most popular" flag on the 3-pack.
Job: convert offer-motivated buyers; great for retargeting.

4Social proof

The 5-star review card

"I used to lie awake for hours. Now I'm out in minutes." ★★★★★

Visual: a real review styled over a calm lifestyle background.
Job: borrow trust — the highest-converting angle once you have reviews.

5Native · UGC-style

The "found it on my feed" flat-lay

"3 things that fixed my sleep — this one surprised me."

Visual: phone-shot flat-lay, imperfect and authentic, not studio-polished.
Job: blend into the feed so it reads as a tip, not an ad.

+Testing note

Launch 3–5 of these in one ad set and let Meta find the winner. Judge on cost-per-purchase after ~50–100 clicks each — don't kill an ad on day one. Keep the listicle as the landing page for all of them.
